01

The policy nobody looked at

The policy was placed 15 years ago by someone who moved on. The estate grew. The coverage didn't. Nobody noticed until it was too late.

Solution

We start with education. Before anything gets placed, we walk through what you've built, what's actually at risk, and what it takes to transfer it the way you intend.

02

Coverage built for who you were

Your estate has grown. New assets, new structures, new family dynamics. But your coverage looks exactly like it did when you first signed.

Solution

We rebuild around the estate you have today — whole life and term coverage, plus fixed and indexed annuities structured around a guaranteed income and long-term wealth transfer plan.

03

Nobody brought the team together

Your CPA, your estate attorney, your wealth manager — they each have a piece of the picture. If nobody has aligned the insurance around the full plan, there are gaps nobody knows about.

Solution

We collaborate with your CPA, attorney, and wealth manager to make sure every piece of the plan is aligned before anything is placed.

04

No trust. No updated will.

Many families have a will that's decades old — or no trust at all. Without one, the estate goes through probate: public, slow, and expensive.

Solution

We coordinate with your estate attorney so insurance, beneficiary designations, and legal documents move together and nothing contradicts itself when the estate transfers.

05

The window is closing

Life insurance gets harder to qualify for every year you wait. For some, it becomes too costly. For others, health changes take it off the table entirely. The window to lock in coverage is smaller than most people think.

Solution

We help you apply while you still qualify — locking in your rate and coverage now, before age or health changes what's available to you.

06

Retirement income that doesn't count against you

Traditional retirement accounts get taxed on withdrawal, and market downturns can shrink your nest egg right when you need income most.

Solution

An Indexed Universal Life (IUL) policy is funded through your premium payments — part covers the cost of insurance, and the rest builds cash value credited based on a market index's performance, with a floor that protects you from market losses. Once that cash value has grown, you can access it income-tax-free through policy loans, creating a flexible income stream in retirement. And if you never need to touch it, your family still receives a death benefit.
